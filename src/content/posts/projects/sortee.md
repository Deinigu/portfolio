Sortee is a small Python and PyGame app that shows how three classic sorting algorithms work, one step at a time. The clip below shows bubble sort and insertion sort running.

## Why

Sorting algorithms are easier to understand when you can watch them work. I built Sortee to show each one step by step, so you can see how it gradually puts the list in order.

## How it works

- **Algorithms.** Insertion sort, bubble sort and selection sort.
- **Visualization.** PyGame draws the list and redraws it at every step of the algorithm.
