## Why

I've studied music since I was a child: piano first, then trombone, playing in a band and music school, until my Software Engineering career made formal studies too demanding. I kept learning on my own, mostly music theory, but I always had the feeling that I'd never composed anything seriously. This jam gave me the excuse to finally do it.

## How I made it

- **Recording.** Reaper as the DAW, with a MIDI keyboard and a microphone.
- **Orchestra.** [BBC Symphony Orchestra Discover](https://www.spitfireaudio.com/bbc-symphony-orchestra-discover) for the main instruments.
- **Atmosphere.** [Spitfire Labs](https://www.spitfireaudio.com/labs/) for spatial and atmospheric music and ambience, plus some free plugins.

## Homemade sound effects

Not every sound came from a plugin. The robot's engine is an electric pencil sharpener, and the sound of selecting a card is a piece of paper.

## What I learned

The hardest part was learning the tools: making the music sound good, mixing it properly, and keeping the soundtrack cohesive with the game's atmosphere. Working with the developers so the music fit the game's mechanics and visuals was new to me too, and seeing the music add to the game made it worth it.
