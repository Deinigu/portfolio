## The 64×64 challenge

LowRezJam's main rule is that the entire experience has to be made with only 64×64 pixels. It was fun working inside that limit. I'm proud of the result, although with more time that month I could have done much more.

## Play it

It's a small, silly game, and you can play it right now in the browser.
